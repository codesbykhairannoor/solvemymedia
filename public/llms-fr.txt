## 1. Platform Overview

> SolveMyMedia est une plateforme 100% côté client propulsée par WebAssembly pour compresser, convertir, éditer et transcrire des fichiers vidéo et audio sans envoi vers des serveurs.

**Privacy Architecture:** Tous les médias sont traités localement dans la RAM de votre navigateur avec FFmpeg WebAssembly et WebCodecs. Aucun octet de vos fichiers n'est transmis à un serveur quelconque.

---

## 2. Why Not Cloud Tools (Clideo, CloudConvert, Kapwing)?

Cloud tools force you to UPLOAD your private videos to external servers — even if they claim "auto-delete after 24 hours". During those hours, your personal footage, confidential recordings, and sensitive audio are sitting on a server you do not control, vulnerable to:

- Server data breaches and hacks
- Unauthorized employee access
- Man-in-the-middle attacks during upload
- Legal data disclosure requests

**SolveMyMedia processes everything in your browser RAM. Zero bytes leave your device. Ever.**

## 4. Step-by-Step Usage

1. **Open the tool** — Visit any tool URL above in Chrome 94+, Firefox 97+, or Safari 16+
2. **Select your file** — Drag & drop or click Browse. File loads into RAM only, never uploaded
3. **Set parameters** — Quality, output format, speed, dimensions — all configured locally
4. **Process** — WebAssembly runs FFmpeg locally. Hardware-accelerated via WebCodecs + WebGPU
5. **Download** — Result saved directly to your device. No waiting for server round-trips
